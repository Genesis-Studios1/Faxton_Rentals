Viewing safety

Extra safety for your property visits.

Meeting someone at an empty property is the riskiest part of renting. Faxton adds simple tools around every in-person viewing, so someone always knows where you are.

  • See who you’re meeting: verified agents carry a Verified badge
  • Share the viewing details with someone you trust on WhatsApp
  • Check in when you arrive and out when you leave. If it runs long, the app asks if you’re OK
  • Emergency button: call 112, alert your contact, tell Faxton or report the agent

Smart Home Match

Tell us your life. We’ll rank the homes.

Instead of endless filtering, answer eight quick questions. Faxton scores every home on how well it fits and shows you why, so the best matches come first.

  • A match score for every home, with the reasons and any flags
  • Rush-hour commute estimates to where you work or study
  • Power and security preferences count too

Eight quick questions

About your budget, space, commute, neighbourhoods, power, security, furnishing and the way you live.

  • Budget
  • Bedrooms
  • Work or study place
  • Neighbourhoods
  • Power
  • Security
  • Furnished or not
  • Your lifestyle

Offers & negotiation

Negotiate a fair rent, on the record.

Offer a yearly rent. The owner can accept, counter or decline, and the whole negotiation stays in one place for both sides.

  • See what you’d save, and your move-in cost at your offer
  • Counter-offers go back and forth inside the app
  • Accepted, declined or withdrawn: the full history is kept

Payments with proof

Pay by bank transfer. Prove it in the app.

Faxton doesn’t collect card details. You pay the owner or agent by bank transfer, then tell us in the app with your bank reference and, if you can, a screenshot of the receipt.

  • The account to pay into, with one-tap copy
  • Upload your proof of payment with the reference
  • Follow the status: Submitted, Confirmed or Rejected
  • A printable receipt once a payment is confirmed. Card payments aren’t available yet

My Home

After you move in, it keeps going.

Once your application is approved, the app opens My Home: your lease dates, rent and service-charge reminders, maintenance requests, documents, notices and a move-out checklist.

  • A lease timeline with a countdown
  • Report a problem and follow it until it’s fixed
  • Rent and service-charge reminders you can add to your calendar
  • Message your landlord from the same place

Neighbourhood information

Know the area before you visit.

Every home comes with an area guide: flood risk, roads, security and power, the average rent nearby, and how long your commute would take. The ratings are Faxton estimates, so we ask you to confirm them at your viewing.

  • Flood riskWhere the water gathers

  • SecurityGates, guards and CCTV
  • ElectricityHours a day, with backup power
  • Average rent nearbyCompared with the area
  • Commute estimatesRush hour and off-peak

Annual rent is only part of it. Faxton shows rent, agency fee, legal fee, caution deposit and service charge together, with the total you’d pay on move-in and what you get back later.
